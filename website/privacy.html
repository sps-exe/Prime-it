Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Overview

Prime-it ("the App") is a productivity tool designed to respect your privacy. We believe your data belongs to you.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • Local Data: Your tasks, settings, and focus history are stored locally on your device using `electron-store`. We do not have access to this data.
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): If you create an account, we use Supabase (a third-party database provider) to sync your profile and subscription status.
  • Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., "app opened", "feature used") to improve the App. This data is not linked to your personal identity.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase: For authentication and database hosting.
  • LemonSqueezy: For processing payments. We do not store your credit card information.
  • OpenAI: If you use the AI Coach feature, your chat messages are sent to OpenAI for processing. They are not used to train OpenAI's models (via API).

4. Your Rights

You can request the deletion of your account and cloud data at any time by contacting us. Local data can be deleted by uninstalling the App and clearing your application support folder.

5. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at support@prime-it.app.
