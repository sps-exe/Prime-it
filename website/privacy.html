Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Overview

Prime It ("the App") is a productivity tool designed to respect your privacy. We believe your data belongs to you.

2. Data Collection

  • Local Data: Task lists and timer settings are stored locally on your device using Electron Store.
  • Account Data: If you create an account, we store your email and subscription status via Supabase (our database provider).
  • Payments: All payments are processed securely by LemonSqueezy. We do not store your credit card information.

3. AI Features

When you use the AI Coach, your task descriptions and productivity stats are sent to OpenAI's API to generate insights. This data is not used to train OpenAI's models (tier dependent) and is transient.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted services:

  • Supabase: Authentication & Database
  • LemonSqueezy: Payments & Subscriptions
  • OpenAI: Intelligence features

5. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us at support@prime-it.app (or your preferred contact method).
