Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Overview

Prime-it ("the App") is a productivity tool designed to respect your privacy. We believe your data belongs to you. This policy outlines what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • Local Data: Your tasks, settings, and focus history are stored primarily on your local device. We do not have access to this raw data by default.
  • Cloud Sync (Encrypted): If you enable Cloud Sync, your tasks and lists are stored securely in our database (Supabase). This data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS), meaning only you can access it.
  • Account Info: We store your email address and subscription status to manage your Pro access.

3. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted services to provide Prime-it's features:

  • Supabase: Provides secure authentication and database hosting.
  • LemonSqueezy: Handles all payments and subscriptions. We never see or store your credit card information.
  • OpenAI: Powers the "AI Coach". When you use this feature, your task data is sent to OpenAI for processing. This data is not used to train their models.

4. Your Rights

You maintain full ownership of your data.

  • You can export or delete your account at any time.
  • You can request a copy of all data synced to our cloud.
  • Local data can be wiped by uninstalling the application.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out to us at support@prime-it.app.
